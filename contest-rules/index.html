Contest Rules

Last updated: July 25, 2025

These Contest Rules explain how quizzes and coin rewards work on 5 Minutes Quiz at our website, operated by our company. By participating in any quiz contest on the Service, you agree to these rules.

1. How Quizzes Work

  • Each quiz contains up to 10 questions drawn from a pool of questions in the selected category.
  • You have 120 seconds to answer all questions in a quiz session. The timer begins immediately after the quiz starts.
  • Questions are shuffled randomly each session, so every play is unique.
  • Once a quiz session begins it cannot be paused.

2. Scoring

  • Correct answer: +50 points.
  • Incorrect answer: −25 points.
  • Points are used to calculate the proportion of coins you earn from the quiz prize pool.
  • Final coin earnings are calculated as: (Your score / Total questions) × Quiz prize pool, rounded to the nearest whole number.

3. Entry Fee

Each quiz has an entry fee in virtual coins. The entry fee is deducted from your coin balance when you start a quiz session. If you do not have enough coins:

  • You will be prompted to watch a rewarded ad to earn +100 coins.
  • After watching the ad, coins are added automatically and you proceed to the quiz.

4. Lifelines

Each quiz session includes four lifelines to help you answer difficult questions. Each lifeline can only be used once per session.

  • 50:50 — Removes two incorrect answer options.
  • Audience Poll — Shows percentage estimates for how a hypothetical audience would vote on the current question.
  • Freeze Timer — Pauses the countdown timer for the current question.
  • Flip Question — Replaces the current question with a different question from the same category.

5. Daily Reward

  • You can claim a free daily reward of 100 coins by watching a short rewarded ad.
  • The daily reward can be claimed up to 2 times per day.
  • The daily limit resets at midnight (UTC).
  • Clicking "No Thanks" on the daily reward prompt will dismiss the offer without playing an ad.

6. Coin Balance — No Real-Money Value

All coins on 5 Minutes Quiz are virtual in-game currency. They have no real monetary value and cannot be redeemed for cash, prizes, or any real-world goods or services. Coin balances are stored locally on your device and are not guaranteed to persist across browsers, devices, or after clearing browser data.

7. Eligibility

  • You must be at least 13 years old to participate.
  • Use of bots, scripts, or any automated tools is strictly prohibited and will result in account suspension.
  • We reserve the right to disqualify any participant who violates these rules or our Terms of Use.

8. Changes to Rules

We may update these Contest Rules at any time. Continued participation in quizzes constitutes acceptance of any updated rules.
