Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how 5 Minutes Quiz, operated by our company ("we", "us", or "our"), uses cookies and similar tracking technologies when you visit our website. By continuing to use the Service, you consent to our use of cookies as described in this policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to site owners. Similar technologies include web beacons, pixel tags, and local storage, which function in comparable ways.

2. Cookies We Use

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Service to function and cannot be switched off. They are usually set only in response to actions you take, such as signing in to your account.

  • Authentication cookies – Used to keep you signed in to your account across sessions.
  • Session state – We use browser localStorage and sessionStorage to store quiz progress, selected categories, and coin balances locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers unless you are signed in.

2.2 Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with the Service, allowing us to improve the user experience. The data is aggregated and anonymous.

  • Google Analytics (GA4) – We use Google Analytics to collect information such as how often users visit the Service, what pages they visit, and what other sites they used prior to visiting. You can learn more at Google's Cookie Policy and opt out at Google Analytics Opt-out.

2.3 Advertising Cookies

We work with advertising partners, including Google, to display ads on the Service. These partners may use cookies and similar technologies to collect information about your activities on the Service and other sites to provide you with targeted advertising.

  • Google Ad Manager / Google AdSense – Google uses cookies such as the DoubleClick cookie to serve ads based on your visit to the Service and other sites on the internet. These cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads tailored to your interests. Google's advertising cookies are governed by Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Google Publisher Tags (GPT) – We use GPT scripts from securepubads.g.doubleclick.net to request and display ads. These scripts may set or read cookies on your device.

3. How to Manage Cookies

  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or delete cookies that have already been set. Refer to your browser's help section for instructions. Note that blocking essential cookies may affect Service functionality.
  • Google Ad personalization: Opt out at Google Ads Settings.
  • Industry opt-outs: Visit aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.com.
  • Google Analytics opt-out: Install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
